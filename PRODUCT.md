# Zhetalis Junior Astronaut
## Platform
Web: browser-based Three.js educational space game.
## Audience and purpose
Junior astronauts learn about space, then train on Earth before Moon, Mars or spaceflight missions. Existing age controls adapt presentation.
## Required behavior
Live hologram briefings use supplied Space_Tech notes and slides. Players remain free to walk and explore. Approach the screen to answer; wrong answers explain and repeat until correct. Practical drills and all quizzes gate launch. Keep authentic NASA assets and source attribution, walk-in shelter, saved progress and existing mission controls.
## Design authorization
User requests a substantial UI redesign using design skills and delegates appearance decisions: JUST BETTER. Prioritize professional, readable controls and visibility of the 3D world.


User revision: Earth academy removed from all playable mission entry paths. New Mars, lunar flyby and lunar surface missions start with launch directly. No preflight hologram, slide viewer, quizzes or drills. Existing surface gameplay and save/resume remain.
