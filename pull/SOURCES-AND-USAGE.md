# Sources and reuse notes

## NASA 3D source archive

Source: https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources
Pinned commit: 11ebb4ee043715aefbba6aeec8a61746fad67fa7
Usage guidance: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/

The repository describes its assets as free and without copyright and directs users to NASA's usage guidance. Keep NASA and contributor credits, original notices and provenance. NASA occasionally hosts third-party material whose rights do not transfer through NASA. Agency insignia, logos and identifiable-person likenesses have additional restrictions; downloaded insignia/emblem models are reference assets, not recommended game branding. Do not imply NASA endorsement. This archive records sources, not a guarantee for every possible commercial use.

## Canadian Space Agency

Terms: https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/terms.asp
CSA permits educational/informational non-commercial reproduction under its stated conditions, including attribution and accuracy. Commercial redistribution generally needs prior written permission unless otherwise specified. Keep these activity PDFs as research/teaching references; do not assume they are unrestricted commercial game art. CSA logos and Government of Canada marks are separately restricted. Saved references are originals, not a claim of affiliation.

## Scientific records

DONKI: https://ccmc.gsfc.nasa.gov/tools/DONKI/
Retain the event identifiers and contributing scientific teams. Follow CCMC's rules of the road for research/publications. These are research products, not operational forecasts.

OSDR: https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/
Cite the study accession, version and associated publication. Dataset metadata contains attribution and publication information. Supporting papers may carry their own licence. Archive possession is not permission to republish a paper as game content. None of these datasets confirms alien Martian life.

RadLab: https://visualization.osdr.nasa.gov/radlab/gui/data-api/
Keep the instrument, location, trajectory, date and original units with each observation. Include the underlying instrument/data-provider credit when publishing analysis. Lunar orbit measurements must not be relabelled as surface measurements.

Mars Trek: https://trek.nasa.gov/tiles/apidoc/trekAPI.html?body=mars
Layer: Mars_Viking_MDIM21_ClrMosaic_global_232m. WMTS capabilities are saved beside the tiles. Imagery is not an elevation model. Preserve source/mission/layer credit when making derivatives.

NASA Image Library: https://images.nasa.gov/
Each original image has search metadata and an asset manifest; the download manifest records its title, creator/centre, description and date. Inspect per-image credits before publishing. Some images feature people, trademarks or third-party contributions.
